# ShopSphere — Business Requirements

## 1. Business Overview

ShopSphere is a large e-commerce platform operating across India.
The company sells products across Electronics, Fashion, Beauty,
Grocery, Home & Kitchen, Sports, Books and Toys.

The company receives data from multiple operational and marketing
systems. Management currently lacks a centralized BI solution for
monitoring business performance.

---

## 2. Business Objective

To build an automated Business Intelligence solution that provides
management with a centralized view of:

- Revenue and profit performance
- Customer behavior
- Product performance
- Marketing effectiveness
- Order and delivery performance
- Payment failures
- Customer support
- Business trends and anomalies

---

## 3. Key Business Questions from different sectors

### Revenue

- What is total revenue?
- How is revenue trending over time?
- Which categories generate the most revenue?
- Which regions generate the most revenue?
- What is driving revenue growth or decline?

### Customers

- How many active customers do we have?
- What percentage of customers are repeat customers?
- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- What is customer retention?
- What is customer lifetime value?

### Products

- Which products and categories perform best?
- Which products have high revenue but low margins?
- Which products have high return rates?

### Marketing

- Which marketing channels generate the most customers?
- Which campaigns generate the highest ROI?
- What is customer acquisition cost?
- What is ROAS?

### Operations

- What percentage of orders are delivered on time?
- Which delivery partners perform best?
- Which cities have the highest delivery delays?
- What is the cancellation rate?

### Customer Support

- What are the most common customer complaints?
- Which issues take the longest to resolve?
- How does customer satisfaction vary by issue?

---

## 4. Primary Stakeholders

# Executive Management
Needs a high-level view of business performance.

# Finance Team
Needs revenue, profit, margin and refund analysis.

Needs campaign performance, CAC and ROAS.

# Operations Team
Needs delivery, cancellation and fulfillment metrics.

## 6. Business Incident

A major checkout experience change was introduced in May 2025.

Management subsequently observed a significant decline in revenue.

The BI system must help identify:

1. When the decline started.
2. Which KPIs changed.
3. Which customer segments were affected.
4. Which regions were affected.
5. Whether payment failures increased.
6. Whether cancellations increased.
7. Whether order volume declined.
8. Whether average order value changed.
9. Whether marketing performance changed.
10. The likely root cause.
11. The estimated financial impact.
12. Recommended corrective actions.
